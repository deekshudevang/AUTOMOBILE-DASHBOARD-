# AUTOVERSE 3D — Vehicle Geometry Engine Architecture (V2)

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The AutoVerse 3D Automotive Authentic Geometry Engine (AAGE V2) provides production-grade, mathematically continuous procedural vehicle geometry for digital twins, real-time WebGL configurators, and automotive CAD visualization.

It completely eliminates:
1. Generic procedural template primitives and box slabs (`BoxGeometry`).
2. Discontinuous panel shutlines, open rocker sills, and floating appendages.
3. Hub occlusions and unnatural wheel arch steps.
4. Identical vehicle silhouette cloning across different automotive classes.

## 2. Core Architectural Pillars

### 2.1 Pure Mathematical Curvature ($C^0$ & $C^1$ Continuity)
All body surfaces are generated using lofted parametric Bezier and spline patches (`createCurvedPatch`) evaluated with smooth trigonometric, polynomial, and normal distribution functions:
- **Longitudinal Flyline**: Expressed as piecewise polynomials $y(z)$ matching OEM silhouette height coordinates.
- **Tumblehome & Waist Tuck**: Modeled as lateral harmonic functions $x(u, v) = \text{side} \cdot (\text{halfW} \cdot 0.90 + \text{waistTuck} + \text{verticalTumble})$.
- **Wheel Arch Harmonization**: Continuous radial cutout with $C^0$ transition into lower rocker sills and front splitters:
  $$\text{archCutoutY} = \begin{cases} 
  r_\text{tyre} + \sqrt{\max(0, r_\text{arch}^2 - dz^2)} \cdot k_\text{clearance} & \text{if } |dz| < r_\text{arch} \\
  r_\text{tyre} - (r_\text{tyre} - y_\text{rocker}) \cdot f_\text{prog}^{0.85} & \text{if } dz \ge r_\text{arch} \\
  r_\text{tyre} - (r_\text{tyre} - y_\text{rocker}) \cdot r_\text{prog}^{0.85} & \text{if } dz \le -r_\text{arch}
  \end{cases}$$
  This formulation guarantees:
  - Minimum radial distance to hub center $\ge 0.331\text{m} > 0.220\text{m}$ (zero hub penetration).
  - Flush shut line closure with rocker sills and bumper skirts at $y_\text{rocker} = \text{gcY} + 0.025\text{m}$.

### 2.2 Watertight Cabin Enclosure & Structural A-Pillars
- **Daylight Opening (DLO)**: Side windows taper cleanly along the raked A-pillar line for $z > Z_\text{ROOF\_FRONT}$ down to $(Z_\text{COWL\_SHUT}, y_\text{belt})$, eliminating artificial sail fins.
- **Dedicated A-Pillar Structural Shells**: Curved 3D patches bridge the windshield flank to the door cant rails and beltline, preventing cockpit light leakage.

## 5. Append-Only 18 Physical Quality Gates Suite

Validation is enforced by `src/geometry/validation/qualityGates.ts` using mathematical invariants:

1. **Overall Dimensions**: $\pm 1\%$ tolerance against verified OEM specs ($L, W, H$).
2. **Ground Contact**: Lowest point $\Delta Y \le 2.5\text{mm}$.
3. **Symmetry**: Lateral left vs right deviation $\Delta X \le 1.0\text{mm}$.
4. **No Floating Geometry**: 0 detached or orphan island meshes.
5. **Manifold Body**: 0 open boundary edges on enclosed monocoques.
6. **Normals & Shading**: 100% computed vertex normals; 0 faceted artifacts.
7. **Componentization**: 4 separable wheel assemblies + $\ge 18$ discrete components.
8. **Triangle Budget**: $10,000 \le \text{triangles} \le 150,000$.
9. **No Primitive Panels**: 0 `BoxGeometry` panels on body surfaces.
10. **Curvature Present**: Double curvature $\ge 3.0\text{mm}$ on hood, roof, and rear deck.
11. **Wheel Arch Fit**: Zero fender vertices inside the $0.22\text{m}$ hub clearance cylinder.
12. **Wheel Visibility**: $\ge 60\%$ unobstructed lateral view of wheel assemblies.
13. **Shut-Line Spacing**: Nominal $2.5\text{mm}$ panel gaps without mesh collision.
14. **Appendage Anchoring**: $\le 2.0\text{mm}$ attachment gap for wings, mirrors, splitters.
15. **Plate & Badge Attachment**: $\le 2.0\text{mm}$ gap to bumper fascia.
16. **Render Sanity**: Viewport surface luminance verified $> 0$.
17. **Provenance Consistency**: Declared tier matches measured dimensional deviation.
18. **Distinctness**: Unique geometry hash and component footprint across models.
