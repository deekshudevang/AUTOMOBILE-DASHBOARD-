## 1. Research Protocol & Sources

### 1. Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992, 2022)
- **Manufacturer**: Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG
- **Model Year**: 2022-Present
- **Body Style**: Two-door rear-engine high-performance coupe
- **Official Specifications**:
  - Length: $4572\text{mm}$ ($4.572\text{m}$)
  - Width: $1900\text{mm}$ ($1.900\text{m}$) excluding mirrors, $2027\text{mm}$ including mirrors

  - Wheelbase: $2457\text{mm}$ ($2.457\text{m}$)
  - Front Track: $1630\text{mm}$, Rear Track: $1600\text{mm}$
  - Ground Clearance: $100\text{mm}$
- **Wheel Fitment**:
  - Front: $275/35\text{ ZR }20$ on $10\text{J} \times 20\text{ ET }45$
  - Rear: $335/30\text{ ZR }21$ on $13\text{J} \times 21\text{ ET }31$
- **Aerodynamic Highlights**: Swan-neck top-hung carbon rear wing with DRS active hydraulic flap, dual front hood extraction nostrils, and front fender louvers.
- **Source**: Porsche Newsroom 911 GT3 RS Technical Data Sheet.
