## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & DEFECT IDENTIFICATION

Users and engineers previously reported an acute inability to see, browse, switch between, and visually inspect the full fleet of 50 vehicles in AutoVerse. Despite previous phases implementing 50 authentic Schema 2.0 vehicle specifications, the application suffered from critical architectural blocks, state leakage, catalog burial, and rendering bypasses.

An unsparing technical audit identified 4 fundamental root causes that prevented vehicle models from being visible and functional.

---

## 2. ROOT CAUSE AUDIT & TECHNICAL DIAGNOSIS

### Defect 1: Priority Inversion in `VehicleRenderer.tsx` (Rendering Bypass)
* **Location**: `src/components/3d/VehicleRenderer.tsx`
* **Defect**: The store flag `useGeometryEngineV2` is `true` by default in production. Inside `VehicleRenderer.tsx`, the component checked `if (useGeometryEngineV2) return null;` at the beginning of the `schema2Body` block. As a direct consequence, whenever a user loaded a Schema 2.0 vehicle specification, `schema2Body` was completely skipped and suppressed. Instead, the renderer fell back to `v2Result` (the legacy monolithic coachwork lofter).
* **Impact**: The 3D scene rendered a static, monolithic exterior mesh. The kinematic opening controller, manufacturer-specific interiors, and engine bays generated by `EditableVehicleAssembler` were never attached to the active scene graph.
* **Resolution**: Replaced the suppression check. Elevated `schema2Body` to Priority 1 whenever `rawSpec?.schemaVersion === '2.0'`. The componentized model with its full kinematic hierarchy and interior assemblies now mounts directly into the active Three.js viewport.

### Defect 2: State Leakage Between Vehicles (`useVehicleStore.ts`)
* **Location**: `src/hooks/useVehicleStore.ts` -> `loadVehicleByFile`
* **Defect**: When switching from Car A to Car B, `loadVehicleByFile` did not reset `openingState`, `selectedComponentId`, or `componentOverrides`. If a user had the scissor doors of a Lamborghini Aventador open and switched to a Porsche 911 or BMW M4, the store carried over `doorFrontLeft = 1.0` and stale component overrides.
* **Impact**: Vehicles loaded in deformed or mismatched states, creating visually broken geometry and broken UI controls.
* **Resolution**: Injected explicit state resets into `loadVehicleByFile`:
  ```ts
  openingState: DEFAULT_OPENING_STATE,
  selectedComponentId: null,
  componentOverrides: {},
  overrideHistory: [{}],
  overrideHistoryIndex: 0,
  cameraPreset: 'isometric',
  cameraTrigger: Date.now()
  ```

### Defect 3: Catalog Clutter & 50 High-Fidelity Vehicles Buried in 450+ Legacy Files
* **Location**: `src/components/library/VehicleLibraryView.tsx`
* **Defect**: The `allFiles` inventory combined the 50 Schema 2.0 vehicles with over 400 legacy GTA vehicle records from `vehicleDatabase.json`. Without tier differentiation, users were forced to scroll through hundreds of placeholder cars with generic procedural meshes.
* **Resolution**: Introduced a dedicated Fleet Tier Toggle at the top of the Vehicle Library:
  - `[⚡ AutoVerse 50 Vehicle Fleet (Schema 2.0)]` (Active by default, filtered to high-fidelity cars)
  - `[📦 All Catalog Files (450+ GTA Models)]` (Legacy exploration)
  - Restored authentic automotive category filters: `Supercar`, `Hypercar`, `Sports Car`, `Luxury Car`, `JDM Performance`, `Muscle`, `Track Car`, `Special / Concept`.

### Defect 4: Inaccessible Vehicle Switching in Studio & Top Context Bar
* **Location**: `src/components/studio/StudioView.tsx`, `src/components/shell/TopContextBar.tsx`
* **Defect**: Neither `StudioView.tsx` nor `TopContextBar.tsx` provided a direct way to browse or switch vehicles. The vehicle title was static plain text, and users had to navigate away to the Library view or manually fiddle with component tabs.
* **Resolution**: 
  1. Created a universal, responsive modal: `src/components/gallery/VehicleSelectionModal.tsx`.
  2. Made the Active Vehicle badge in `TopContextBar` an interactive button with a "Switch" pill.
  3. Added a dedicated `[🚗 Select Vehicle]` button directly in the top HUD of `StudioView.tsx`.
  4. Added a `[Browse All 50 Fleet Models...]` button in the left sidebar of `CustomizationPanel.tsx`.
