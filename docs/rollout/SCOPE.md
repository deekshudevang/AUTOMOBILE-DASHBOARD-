## 2. In-Scope Real Production Vehicles (58 Models)
The 58 real production vehicles are classified into 6 primary automotive styling archetypes:

### Archetype 1: `rear_engine_coupe` (Lead: Porsche 911 GT3 RS 992)
- Distinctive teardrop fastback greenhouse
- Muscular widebody rear quarter haunches enclosing rear-mounted engine
- Low sloping front hood flanked by raised torpedo fender crests
- Active aerodynamic rear wing assembly

### Archetype 2: `mid_engine_supercar` (Lead: Ferrari 488 Pista)
- Cab-forward canopy profile with low aerodynamic wedge nose
- Deep scalloped side air intake scoops behind doors feeding twin-turbo intercoolers
- Front hood aerodynamic S-Duct channel
- Flying buttresses flanking transparent glass engine deck lid
- Dual circular taillights & high-caliber exhaust exits

### Archetype 3: `mid_engine_hypercar` (Lead: Lamborghini Aventador SVJ)
- Extreme low-slung, razor-sharp faceted wedge architecture ($H \le 1.14\text{m}$)
- Hexagonal aeronautical design language throughout
- Multi-tier ALA 2.0 active aerodynamic carbon rear wing
- High-mounted center dual exhaust exiting between taillights
- Slatted rear engine decklouvers

### Archetype 4: `front_engine_gt` (Lead: Aston Martin DBS Superleggera / Ferrari 812)
- Long sculpted bonnet with front mid-mounted V12/V8
- Set-back 2+2 grand touring cockpit greenhouse
- Muscular rear shoulder lines with short, taut fastback decklid
- Deep front grille and fender side air breathers

### Archetype 5: `sports_sedan` (Lead: BMW M3/M5, Alfa Romeo Giulia Quadrifoglio)
- Three-box silhouette with upright 4-door greenhouse
- Hofmeister kink rear door window graphic
- Chiseled body shoulder swage line and dual twin exhaust diffusers

### Archetype 6: `suv_crossover` (Lead: Lamborghini Urus, Porsche Cayenne Turbo GT)
- Elevated ride height with chiseled coupe-SUV roofline
- Geometric protective wheel arch claddings and massive front radiator intakes
- Quad exhaust outlets and aggressive rear roof spoiler

## 3. Truth Contract & Quality Tiers
- **Tier A (Reference-Verified)**: Blueprint orthographic alignment $\le 15\text{mm}$.
- **Tier B (Spec-Accurate)**: OEM verified overall dimensions, wheelbase, track widths, wheel/tire specs $\le 20\text{mm}$.
- **Tier C / Class D (Approximate Fallback)**: Legacy procedural template carrying mandatory approximation notice.
